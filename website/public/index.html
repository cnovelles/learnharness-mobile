A tutor that starts from what you already know.

OrbiTutor finds where your understanding ends, plans a path to your goal and teaches it one step at a time. Start a lesson on your Mac. Continue it on your iPhone.

How it works

One lesson, four steps.

  1. 1

    Find the starting point

    A few short questions show what you already understand. Your confidence and your own words count, not only right or wrong.

  2. 2

    Map the path

    OrbiTutor builds a plan from your starting point to your goal. You can see the plan, and it changes when you do.

  3. 3

    Learn step by step

    Each idea builds on one that you already have. You predict, you read, and you check. A confusion gets fixed before the next step.

  4. 4

    Practice until it sticks

    Practice sessions give new problems that need the right idea at the right time. What you learn goes into a knowledge graph for the next lesson.

Mac and iPhone

Your Mac teaches. Your iPhone comes along.

  • The Mac app runs the lessons

    Your courses, sessions and knowledge graph stay in a folder on your Mac.

  • The iPhone app pairs with your Mac

    Scan a code on the Mac once. Then read, answer and explore your graph from your phone, on your own network.

  • Bring your own AI model

    Use an API key, a supported subscription or a local model server. There is no OrbiTutor account and no subscription.

About

Built on LearnHarness

OrbiTutor is based on LearnHarness, the open-source learning app by Amos Blomqvist. The way OrbiTutor teaches comes from LearnHarness: the measure, plan, teach and practice loop, and the knowledge graph that carries what you learn.

OrbiTutor extends that work. It adds an iPhone app that pairs with the Mac, and more changes on top. It follows each LearnHarness release and keeps the same open-source license.

Thank you to Amos Blomqvist and the LearnHarness contributors. OrbiTutor is an independent project. The LearnHarness project does not make or endorse it.
